Privacy Policy — Everstone Local SEO Suite

Chrome extension by Everstone Digital · Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. What the extension is

Everstone Local SEO Suite is a private tool for local-SEO agencies and their team members. It audits local business listings and websites, tracks local rankings, finds and contacts business leads, and prepares reports and proposals. Only people the agency’s administrator has added can sign in.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not record your general browsing. Outside the tool’s own features, websites you visit are not logged.
  • We do not read your e-mail inbox, except — when you press “Check replies” — reading replies to e-mails you sent with the tool (Gmail read-only permission, optional).
  • We do not run code downloaded from the internet; all code ships inside the extension.

4. Google user data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the feature you asked for and is not transferred to others except as needed to provide that feature, to comply with law, or with your consent.

5. Sharing

Data goes only to: your agency’s own Access Server, the Google services you connect, the AI / data providers whose keys your agency adds, the map look-up services named above (only the place or keyword typed), and the people you choose to send reports or messages to.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Data in the browser stays until you delete it (Settings → Backup & restore, or removing the extension). Your agency administrator controls the data on the agency’s Access Server and its Google Sheet; ask them to delete your records.

7. Security

Requests to the Access Server are signed per session, time-stamped and bound to the device that signed in. Connections use HTTPS.
